# English app-icon example — Rillnote

> Fictional teaching case. It is a concept specification, not a finished identity or trademark clearance.

## Request

“Design an app icon for Rillnote, a private note app that turns scattered thoughts into an ordered stream. It should feel calm and useful, not playful. Explore an `R`, a flowing path, and a page, but avoid three icons pasted together.”

## Good response shape

- Mode: `design`
- Primary context: app icon, launcher grid, favicon
- Assumption: the name is pronounced as written and the first release uses Latin script

| Meaning | Visual structure | Language structure |
|---|---|---|
| Rillnote | stem, bowl, diagonal leg | uppercase `R` |
| Ordered stream | one continuous bend, changing direction | “rill” as a small stream |
| Note | page edge, fold, open counter | “note” |

Keep `R` and flow as the two core signals. Treat the page as a possible structural cue, not a required third object.

## Concept families

| Family | Method | Shared structural move |
|---|---|---|
| Ribbon R | Shared stroke | One continuous ribbon forms the `R` stem, bowl, and outgoing stream. |
| Folded counter | Negative space | The `R` counter is opened as a page-turn void rather than receiving a separate page icon. |
| Riverbank bowl | Shared contour | The outside of the `R` bowl also describes one bank of a narrowing current. |
| Redirected leg | Rotation and reframing | The diagonal leg becomes the stream’s change of direction while remaining necessary to read `R`. |
| Two-bank R | Modules, gaps, and outer silhouette | Two rounded banks create an `R` through their gap; the group still has one dominant silhouette. |
| Page-ribbon glyph | Glyph construction | A folded strip is redrawn on an `R` skeleton so page and letter use the same stroke inventory. |

Do not present six cosmetic variations of one ribbon as six families.

## Selection

Select **Ribbon R** if it wins brand fit, fusion quality, 16 px survival, one-color reproduction, and similarity-risk review. Lock the `R` silhouette, continuous-ribbon relationship, dominant bend, uniform fill logic, and open counter before polishing.

Production direction:

- draw a one-color SVG on a square artboard;
- keep the outer icon silhouette clear without a decorative container;
- reserve enough safe area for common app-icon masks;
- make the counter and direction change survive at 16–32 px;
- test the mark without the wordmark before adding color.

## Honest QA boundary

This text-only example has not rendered or inspected an artifact. Report visual QA as `not run`, not `pass`. A real delivery must inspect the actual output at 16, 24, 32, 64, and 128 px, in black, reversed white, grayscale, and silhouette-only. Record similarity concerns separately from trademark clearance.
